2. Information we process2. 我们处理的信息

  • Account and connection information: local business account identifiers, roles and department access; authorized Page/organization IDs and names; granted permissions; encrypted access/refresh tokens and expiry dates; and an app-scoped authorizing-user ID where the platform supplies one. We do not receive your Facebook or LinkedIn password.
  • Company-owned materials and publishing records: product/company documents and images supplied by authorized users, instructions, language/market/timezone choices, drafts, approvals, schedules, platform post IDs, and delivery results.
  • Authorized engagement: comments, reaction types, platform identifiers, timestamps, and display names only where available through approved APIs and permissions; and platform-provided aggregate performance counts. We do not collect private messages, friend lists, or unrelated member profiles through the current social connection.
  • Business and privacy correspondence: names, business contact details, product requirements, and messages you or an authorized representative provide. Separately obtained business contacts are kept distinct from platform engagement; a social interaction is not permission to add someone to a marketing list.
  • Technical information: request IP addresses, time, browser/user-agent information, access/error logs, and necessary security/session information. These legal pages contain no advertising pixels or third-party analytics.

We do not ask for identity documents, financial account details, health data, biometric identifiers, or other sensitive information for ordinary social-management use. Please do not place such information in uploads, prompts, or deletion emails.

  • 账号和连接信息：本系统商务账号标识、角色、部门权限；已授权主页/组织的 ID、名称、权限范围、加密的访问/刷新令牌及到期时间；以及平台提供时的授权用户应用范围标识。我们不会获取 Facebook 或 LinkedIn 密码。

3. Purposes and legal grounds3. 使用目的与处理依据

We use the minimum necessary information to authenticate and scope access; connect authorized Pages; prepare, review and schedule business content; report publishing status; display authorized engagement and performance; handle business/privacy requests; prevent misuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable, our grounds are the steps you request or the business service agreement, your consent for optional/permission-based features, legitimate interests in secure business administration and responding to inquiries where permitted, and legal obligations. A platform permission is not a substitute for any separate consent required by law. We obtain additional consent or provide a separate notice when required.

We do not sell or rent personal data or platform data, use it for unrelated advertising, build member profiles or credit/employment scores, or make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not bypass platform controls or scrape personal data from Facebook or LinkedIn.

我们仅在必要范围内将信息用于登录和权限隔离、授权主页连接、商务内容准备与审核排期、发布状态反馈、获准互动和效果统计、商务及隐私请求处理、安全防滥用及履行法定义务。

在适用情况下，处理依据包括您请求的操作或商务服务约定、可选/授权功能所需同意、法律允许的安全运营与询盘处理等合法利益，以及法定义务。平台授权不能替代法律要求的单独同意；需要额外同意或告知时应先完成。

我们不出售、出租个人或平台数据，不用于无关广告，不建立会员画像或信用、就业评分，不作出对个人产生法律或类似重大影响的纯自动化决定，也不绕过平台限制抓取 Facebook 或 LinkedIn 个人数据。

4. Facebook and LinkedIn permissions4. Facebook 与 LinkedIn 授权

Connecting an account requires the platform’s official authorization flow and appropriate Page management authority. The consent screen identifies requested access. Publishing, engagement reading and aggregate reporting depend on permissions actually granted; granting one does not guarantee the others. Only authorized management-team members receive access within our department/role controls.

We send approved text, selected images and links to the chosen platform when an authorized user submits a publishing schedule. Published information is then handled by that platform and may be public. Revoking authorization or disconnecting stops future access through that connection; it does not necessarily erase records already received or posts already published. Use the data deletion instructions to request removal of information we hold.

We use platform data only for the authorized management purpose and the platform-approved use case. Applicable platform restrictions take precedence over any broader wording in this policy; we do not rely on this policy as permission for a prohibited use.

连接账号需经过平台官方授权流程，并具有相应主页管理权限。授权页面会展示请求的权限。发布、互动读取和汇总统计分别取决于实际授予的权限，获得其中一项不代表获得全部功能。本系统按部门和角色，仅向获授权的主页管理团队开放相应数据。

授权用户提交发布排期后，我们会将已审核正文、所选图片和链接发送至指定平台。已发布信息可能公开，并由相应平台按其规则处理。撤销授权或断开连接会停止通过该连接继续访问，但不一定删除已取得的记录或已发布帖子。如需删除本系统持有的信息，请查看数据删除说明。

平台数据仅用于授权管理目的和平台批准的用途。本政策中的一般表述不能扩大平台允许范围；平台限制更严格时适用更严格要求。

5. AI features and your control5. AI 功能与用户控制

AI-assisted authoring: when an authorized user selects this feature, the chosen company-owned facts/materials and writing instructions are sent to the configured AI API to draft content in the selected language. DeepSeek is the currently configured authoring provider. Users must not include unnecessary personal data or material they have no right to share. Generated content may be inaccurate: users can edit it and must review it before approving publication. Scheduling is not unrestricted permission for future unreviewed AI posts.

Engagement classification: optional analysis is disabled by default and must remain disabled unless the chosen processor, written processing arrangements, platform permissions, and applicable notice/consent meet the platform’s requirements. Permitted analysis is limited to topic/sentiment categories and prioritizing human review. Member identity is not included in the classification prompt. Third-party LinkedIn content is not used to generate or rewrite replies, posts or messages, train models, or create CRM prospect lists.

We do not train AI models on platform data or authorize an AI provider to use restricted platform data for its own training or unrelated purposes. An API “no storage” request alone is not a substitute for a binding processing arrangement. Reports about unsafe or inaccurate AI content can be sent to autobestmaple@gmail.com.

AI 辅助撰写：授权用户选用此功能时，所选择的公司自有事实、资料及写作要求会发送至已配置的 AI 接口，生成对应语言草稿。当前配置的文案服务提供商为 DeepSeek。不得输入不必要的个人信息或无权对外提供的材料。AI 输出可能有误，用户可以编辑，并须在批准发布前核查。定时任务不代表允许后续未经审核的 AI 内容无限自动发布。

互动分类：该可选分析默认关闭；只有选定处理方、书面处理约定、平台权限及必要告知/同意均符合平台要求时才可启用。允许的用途仅限主题/情感分类和人工处理排序，分类提示中不加入会员身份。第三方 LinkedIn 内容不用于生成或改写回复、帖子、私信，不用于模型训练或生成 CRM 拓客名单。

我们不以平台数据训练 AI，也不授权处理方以受限平台数据进行自身训练或无关使用。接口请求中的“不存储”设置不能替代有约束力的数据处理安排。AI 内容问题可发送至 autobestmaple@gmail.com。

7. Retention and deletion7. 保留与删除

Retention depends on purpose, data type, authorization status, applicable law and platform limits. The shortest applicable platform limit prevails over a general business retention period. Access tokens remain encrypted only while needed for the connection and are cleared on disconnection; expired or revoked permissions stop processing through that connection.

  • Other members’ LinkedIn profile/display information: a cache of no more than 24 hours.
  • Member-level social activity and its associated analysis: a cache of no more than 48 hours; content changes or deletion invalidate the old analysis. These limits are not permission to keep refreshing or archiving data for an unrelated purpose.
  • Non-member aggregate Page reporting: no more than one year. Other organization data is subject to the applicable shorter platform limit.
  • Company-supplied materials, approved publishing records and independent business correspondence: only as needed for the stated business purpose, an applicable agreement, legal obligations or handling a genuine dispute. We explain any applicable retention when responding to a request.

When data is no longer needed or deletion is required, it is deleted or irreversibly anonymized as permitted. We do not use backups to evade platform deletion deadlines: identifiable copies must be addressed where required, and deletion restrictions must be reapplied before restored data is used. Any narrow legal preservation must be explained and cannot be used for marketing.

保留期限取决于目的、信息类型、授权状态、适用法律和平台限制。平台要求的更短期限优先于一般业务保留期限。访问令牌仅在连接需要期间加密保存，断开连接时清除；权限到期或撤销后停止通过该连接处理。

  • 其他 LinkedIn 会员的资料/显示信息：缓存不超过 24 小时。
  • 会员级社交活动及其关联分析：缓存不超过 48 小时；内容变更或删除后使旧分析失效。这不代表可为无关目的反复刷新或归档数据。
  • 不含会员明细的主页汇总统计：不超过一年。其他组织数据适用相应平台更短期限。
  • 公司自行提供的资料、已审核发布记录及独立商务沟通：仅在实现所述目的、履行适用约定/法定义务或处理真实争议所需期间保留。处理请求时会说明适用保留情况。

不再需要或依法/依平台应删除时，按允许方式删除或不可逆匿名化。不得以备份规避平台删除期限；必要时处理可识别备份副本，恢复数据投入使用前重新应用删除限制。确需法定保留的有限信息会说明理由，不用于营销。

8. Security and international processing8. 安全与跨境处理

We use HTTPS, encrypted social credentials, role/department access controls, private material storage, and restricted operational access. These measures reduce risk but do not make internet or email transmission risk-free. Report a suspected incident to autobestmaple@gmail.com without sending passwords or tokens.

Our team operates in China and the current Alibaba Cloud application deployment is in Singapore. Meta, LinkedIn, Google and an enabled AI provider may process information in other countries under their own arrangements. DeepSeek’s published privacy notice describes processing in China; the applicable API agreement and actual feature configuration must also be checked. These are not promises of local-only storage.

Where cross-border processing requires a lawful transfer mechanism, assessment, separate notice or consent, we must complete it before the relevant processing. Contact us to ask about the arrangements applicable to your information. This policy does not itself create a transfer exemption or replace required safeguards.

我们使用 HTTPS、社媒凭证加密、角色/部门权限、私有资料存储及受限运维访问来降低风险，但互联网和电子邮件传输不能保证绝对安全。安全事件线索可发送至 autobestmaple@gmail.com，请勿附带密码或令牌。

团队在中国运营，当前阿里云应用部署于新加坡。Meta、LinkedIn、Google 及启用的 AI 提供方可能依各自安排在其他地区处理信息。DeepSeek 公开隐私说明描述了在中国处理的情况，具体仍需核对适用 API 约定和实际功能配置；本政策不承诺所有数据仅在您当地保存。

跨境处理依法需要传输机制、评估、单独告知或同意时，应在相关处理前完成。您可联系我们了解自身信息适用的安排；本政策不能替代所需保障，也不构成跨境处理豁免。

9. Your choices and privacy rights9. 您的选择与权利

Depending on applicable law, you may request information/access, correction, deletion, restriction, an available portable copy, withdrawal of consent, or objection to processing, including direct marketing. We do not penalize people for exercising applicable privacy rights. You may also contact the competent data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

Send your request to autobestmaple@gmail.com. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request and your authority; we do not need your password, OAuth token or full identity-document number. We aim to respond within 30 calendar days; shorter mandatory legal/platform deadlines prevail. If more information, a lawful exception or an allowed extension is needed, we explain the reason and the next step.

You can revoke platform permission independently, and authorized administrators can disconnect a Page in MapleMail. For full deletion scope and what disconnection does not remove, see Data deletion instructions. Marketing opt-out information may be minimally retained solely to avoid contacting you again, where lawful; it does not authorize continued platform-data retention.

根据适用法律，您可申请知情/访问、更正、删除、限制处理、可用的可携带副本、撤回同意或反对处理（包括直接营销）。我们不会因行使适用隐私权利而对您不利。您也可向有管辖权的数据保护主管机关投诉。

请发送请求至 autobestmaple@gmail.com。我们可能要求与请求相称的核验信息和代理权限证明，但无需您提供密码、OAuth 令牌或完整证件号码。我们争取在 30 个自然日内答复；法律或平台要求更短期限的，以更短期限为准。需要补充信息、适用合法保留例外或依法延期时，会说明理由与后续步骤。

您可独立在平台撤销权限，获授权管理员也可在 MapleMail 断开主页连接。完整删除范围及断开连接的限制见数据删除说明。法律允许时，可为避免再次联系而最小化保留退订标识；这不授权继续保留受限平台数据。
