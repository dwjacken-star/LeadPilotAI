1. Request deletion — no local account required1. 提交删除申请——无需本系统账号

Anyone whose personal or platform-linked data we hold may request deletion, including a person who only interacted with a managed Facebook or LinkedIn post. You do not need to log into MapleMail, authorize a new app, or pay a fee to submit an ordinary request.

Email autobestmaple@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request — BESTMAPLE / MapleMail. You may also request access, correction, restriction, an available copy, or withdrawal of consent at the same address.

Include only what helps locate the data: Facebook or LinkedIn; the relevant Page/profile/post URL or app-scoped ID if known; the email used to contact us if relevant; the scope you want deleted; and a reply address. If you act for someone else, explain your authority. A precise URL is enough to start; do not send a password, access token or identity-document copy.

2. Stop future access or publishing2. 停止后续访问或发布

  • Facebook: in Facebook settings, find Apps and Websites or Business Integrations, select the connected application and remove its access. Menu labels vary by account type. See Facebook application settings.
  • LinkedIn: go to Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Other applications → Permitted services and remove the service. See LinkedIn’s instructions.
  • MapleMail administrators: in Social marketing → Social accounts, disconnect the relevant Page. Only a person with the necessary management permission can do this.

Revocation and deletion are different. Disconnecting clears local connection tokens, stops future connection use and clears its local interaction cache, but may leave historical publishing records and account metadata. A request already accepted by a platform may finish. To remove remaining information, email us; we do not tell you that everything has been deleted merely because a Page was disconnected.

  • Facebook：在设置中找到“应用和网站”或“商务集成”，选择已连接应用并移除权限；菜单会因账号类型变化。可查看 Facebook 应用设置。
  • LinkedIn：进入“设置与隐私 → 数据隐私 → 其他应用 → 允许的服务”，移除相应服务。参阅 LinkedIn 官方说明。
  • MapleMail 管理员：在“社媒营销 → 社媒账号”中断开对应主页；需要相应管理权限。

撤权与删除不同。断开连接会清除本地连接令牌、停止后续使用该连接并清理关联互动缓存，但可能保留历史发布记录和账号元数据；平台已受理的操作也可能完成。删除剩余信息请另行联系我们，不会仅因断开主页就宣称全部删除完毕。

3. How we handle a request3. 我们如何办理

  1. Locate and verify. We identify the relevant records and use proportionate verification. We may ask you to confirm control of a relevant email or Page, or supply a post link; we do not ask for your platform password.
  2. Limit further processing. Where appropriate, we stop the affected connection, scheduling, synchronization or analysis while processing the request.
  3. Delete or explain. We remove information within the confirmed scope that we control, including relevant tokens, linked identifiers/metadata, cached interactions and associated analyses, unless a specific lawful exception applies. Processor-held copies must also be addressed where applicable.
  4. Reply with the outcome. We explain what was removed, any verification still needed, and the reason/scope of any lawful retention. You may ask us to review an incomplete or refused response.

We act promptly and aim to respond within 30 calendar days. Shorter applicable legal or platform deadlines take priority. If a permitted extension is necessary, we explain it and its reason. A deletion email is handled by people; this page is an instructions page, not an automated callback or an instant status checker.

  1. 定位与核验：确定关联记录，按必要、适度原则核验身份或代理权限。可能需要确认相关邮箱/主页控制权或补充帖子链接，不需要提供平台密码。
  2. 限制后续处理：适当情况下，在办理期间停止受影响连接、排期、同步或分析。
  3. 删除或说明：删除经确认范围内由我们控制的信息，包括相关令牌、关联标识/元数据、互动缓存及分析；存在具体合法例外时说明理由，必要时协调处理方的副本。
  4. 反馈结果：说明已删除内容、仍需补充的核验信息，以及依法保留的范围和原因。您可要求复核不完整或拒绝处理的答复。

我们会尽快办理，争取 30 个自然日内答复；法律或平台要求更短期限的，优先适用。依法允许且需要延期时，会说明原因。本邮箱请求由人工处理；本网页是操作说明，不是自动回调接口或即时状态查询器。

4. What deletion does and does not remove4. 删除范围与限制

Our deletion response concerns data held by us and applicable processors. It does not delete your Facebook or LinkedIn account, erase content hosted by those platforms, or remove copies other people lawfully control. Delete platform-hosted posts/comments in the original platform or use its support channel. A separate product order or direct business correspondence may have its own lawful retention basis; we explain any applicable exception.

Where permitted, a minimal opt-out marker may be kept solely to prevent future unwanted business email, not for profiling or further marketing. Backups are access-restricted and must not be used to avoid required deletion or retention limits; required erasure also applies to identifiable copies, and deletion decisions must be reapplied before restored data is used.

If you have never connected a Page but your comment or other information was processed, use the same email route. If we find no matching data after reasonable checks, we explain that outcome rather than require you to create an account.

我们处理由我们及适用受托方持有的数据，不会因此注销您的 Facebook/LinkedIn 账号、自动删除平台上的帖子/评论，或删除他人依法控制的副本。平台内容应在原平台删除或向平台申请处理。独立产品订单或直接商务沟通可能存在各自合法保留依据，适用时会说明。

法律允许时可最小化保留退订标识，仅用于避免再次发送不受欢迎的商务邮件，不用于画像或继续营销。备份须受访问限制，不能用于规避删除和保留期限；必要删除同样涉及可识别副本，恢复数据投入使用前须重新应用删除决定。

即使从未连接主页，只要您的评论等信息曾被处理，也可使用同一邮箱申请。经合理核查未发现匹配数据时会说明结果，不要求您为此创建账号。
